- Change: "Share anonymous usage and diagnostic data" is on by default. It sends anonymous details such as app version, OS, region and device count, plus diagnostic records without content; no clipboard content or names are collected. Turn it off under Privacy in Settings to stop sending and clear pending data
- New: Direct device sync (experimental). Create an encrypted space or join one with a six-digit invitation code to sync across networks, without sharing a Wi-Fi network; Standard sync stays the default and you can switch at any time
- New: Space device management to view each device's connection status, reconnect, or remove it; pairing shows a matching confirmation code on both devices, and custom relay servers are supported
- New: Shares are sent over the selected sync method
- New: Video support when capturing or picking from the photo library
- New: Skippable welcome tour and a confirmation step before connecting by QR code
- Improvement: Toggle diagnostic logging and export diagnostic reports; each log keeps up to 5 MiB
- Change: Phones are locked to portrait; tablets can still rotate freely
- New: Material 3 bottom navigation with redesigned Clipboard, Devices and Settings pages
- New: In-app camera that switches between photo and video
- Improvement: History grouped by day, tap for details, double-tap to copy; filters now live in search
- Improvement: Word selection is now its own page with draggable range handles
- Improvement: Content shared from other apps is saved to history first, then confirmed on a full-screen share page, so closing the page never loses it
- Fix: Sharing a file could get stuck and block later shares, and sharing a file to UniClip itself emptied its content
